# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

This project's issue tracker and discussions exist for questions,
technical discussion, and bug reports about the codebase — not for
active open-source collaboration (see [`LICENSE`](./LICENSE) and
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](./CONTRIBUTING.md) for why). Even at that smaller
scale, everyone interacting here — asking a question, reporting an
issue, or discussing the architecture — is expected to do so
respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially when disagreeing about a
  technical decision.
- Stay on topic: this repository's discussions are about the codebase,
  its architecture, and its documentation.
- Accept that not every suggestion will be acted on — this is a
  personal reference project with a specific scope (see
  [`README.md`'s Known Limitations](./README.md#known-limitations) for
  what's deliberately out of scope already).

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or
  political attacks.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate
  in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to
**daweilin7689@gmail.com**. All reports will be reviewed and result in
whatever response is deemed appropriate — from a clarifying comment up
to blocking further interaction with this repository.
